These services have different runtimes and responsibilities. Confirm the environment before starting a job or retrying a write.

## Scheduled data updates

Use [scheduled jobs](/pipeline/supporting/scheduled-jobs) to investigate a missed update, partial failure, or retry. A scheduler startup message does not prove that a job completed.

### Investigate a missed update

Follow the [missed-update procedure](/pipeline/supporting/scheduled-jobs#investigate-a-missed-update) before retrying a job that can write external systems.

## News-report integration

The scheduler can trigger PIP-NFR, and Pipeline's emailer can deliver its callback. PIP-NFR remains separate from Pipeline and PIP. Use the [PIP-NFR guide](/pip-nfr/overview) for report generation and delivery limits.

## Direct S3 uploads

Use [direct S3 uploads](/pipeline/supporting/uploads) for metadata checks and the multipart POST transfer. Do not replace the returned POST form with a presigned PUT.

## Edge tracker behavior

Use [edge tracker reporting](/pipeline/supporting/trackers) for battery modes, reporting limits, and fleet configuration. For the service-side machine and fault workflow, use [rental diagnostics](/pipeline/services/rental-diagnostics).

### Compare the Rental Tracker wiki with the firmware

The [tracker guide](/pipeline/supporting/trackers#compare-the-rental-tracker-wiki-with-the-firmware) distinguishes wiki descriptions from inspected firmware and explains the conflicting diagnostic timing.
